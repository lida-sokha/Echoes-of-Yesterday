Echoes in the Lab

Echoes in the Lab is a story-driven first-person puzzle adventure game developed using Unity.
The game focuses on emotional storytelling, environmental exploration, and light puzzle mechanics, set inside an abandoned scientific laboratory.

- Game Overview

Title: Echoes in the Lab

Genre: Puzzle Adventure / Narrative Mystery

Platform: PC (Windows)

Engine: Unity 3D

Camera: First-Person

Target Audience: Ages 13+

The player explores a sealed research laboratory, solves puzzles to unlock new areas, and uncovers the truth behind a tragic experiment involving someone they deeply loved.

- Story Summary

You were a scientist working alongside your research partner — someone you loved deeply.
Together, you were developing a breakthrough technology meant to save lives.

But the final experiment went wrong.

Your partner died

You survived

The laboratory was shut down by the government

Months later, unable to move on, you return to the abandoned lab to uncover the truth:

Was it really an accident?

Was something hidden from you?

Did your partner know the risks?

As you explore the lab, you discover logs, notes, and recordings left behind.
In the final chamber, you learn the truth: your partner knowingly sacrificed themselves to protect you.

The game ends with acceptance, closure, and letting go.

- Gameplay Features
Player Abilities

Move using W, A, S, D

Look around using Mouse

Interact with objects using E

Read notes, logs, and terminals

Solve environmental puzzles

Game Structure

The game is linear and room-based, consisting of five main areas:

Entrance

Power Room

Chemical Lab

Security Room

Experiment Chamber (Finale)

Each room contains:

One main puzzle

Environmental storytelling

Story logs or recordings

There are no fail states — players can retry puzzles freely.

- Puzzle Examples

Power Routing Puzzle
Restore electricity by connecting wires in the correct order.

Chemical Lab Password Puzzle
Discover a password using notes collected earlier.

Final Machine Activation
Activate the core device using information gathered throughout the game.

Solving the final puzzle triggers the ending cutscene.
